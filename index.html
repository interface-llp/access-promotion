Health & Safety at Access Promotions

At Access Promotions, the health, safety and wellbeing of our employees, contractors, clients, visitors and venue partners are integral to the way we plan and deliver exhibition and event projects.

Our approach is based on identifying hazards before work begins, assessing project-specific risks and implementing practical controls throughout fabrication, transportation, installation and dismantling.

Depending on the nature and location of each project, these controls include:

01
Site-specific risk assessments and method statements
02
Safety induction and toolbox talks for employees and contractors
03
Appropriate personal protective equipment
04
Controls for work at height, lifting operations and manual handling
05
Electrical, fire and machinery-safety precautions
06
Good housekeeping and safe access to work areas
07
Emergency preparedness and incident-reporting procedures
08
Compliance with applicable venue requirements and statutory obligations
09
Periodic inspection, supervision and corrective-action follow-up

We work to meet applicable Indian occupational health and safety legislation, local authority requirements and the safety rules established by exhibition venues and event organisers. We also seek to align our safety-management practices with the principles of ISO 45001, including leadership responsibility, worker participation, risk-based planning and continual improvement.

We recognise that effective safety management requires ongoing effort. We therefore remain committed to strengthening employee competency, contractor coordination, documentation and performance monitoring as our operations develop.
